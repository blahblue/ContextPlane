# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ContextPlane welcomes technical disagreement and rigorous review. Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas, code, architecture, and evidence rather than people.

Expected behavior includes:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and changing position when evidence warrants it;
- respecting different levels of experience;
- avoiding harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or targeted hostility;
- protecting private or sensitive information encountered during collaboration.

## Scope

This standard applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation for repeated or serious misconduct.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately rather than posting personal details publicly.
